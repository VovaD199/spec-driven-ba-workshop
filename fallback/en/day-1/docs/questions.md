# Open questions about the project (example)

> FALLBACK EXAMPLE for Day 1 (step 10). Compiled from the structure of the lf-client app; the questions are open ("why", "who", "what happens if…"), with no hint of the answer. Read it, edit it to match your own understanding of the project (remove what does not interest you, add your own) and write it into your `docs/questions.md`. See `fallback/en/README.md`.

| ID | Question | Why (which decision depends on the answer) | Who can answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q-01 | Who are the main visitors of the site and what do they most often want to do? | Determines which pages and forms matter | Product owner |
| Q-02 | What happens to the data from the contact form: who sees it, where is it stored and for how long? | It is personal data of visitors; privacy and retention requirements depend on the answer | Legal / personal data officer |
| Q-03 | Why do the "Contacts" form and the partnership block send data through different API routes? | It may be legacy; which route to keep depends on the answer | Tech lead |
| Q-04 | Where do page texts (in uk and en) come from and who edits them? | Affects the content change process | Content manager |
| Q-05 | What should happen when a user submits the form and email is not working? | No email is built right now: behaviour on failure is needed | Product owner |
